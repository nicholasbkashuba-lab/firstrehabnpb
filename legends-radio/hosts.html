On-Air Personalities

The people behind the music.

Live and local means real voices you get to know — the hosts who make Legends 100.3 feel like the best table in the room.

Home/The people behind the music.
J&R
Weekday Mornings

Jill & Rich Switzer

The Morning Lounge
Weekdays · 6–10 AM

South Florida's first couple of the Songbook open every weekday. Jill is one of the region's most sought-after vocalists — a songwriter and published author — while Rich is a gifted pianist and composer. Together they also perform their acclaimed cabaret, Supper Club with Jill & Rich.

MM
Weekday Middays

Mike McGann

Middays with Mike McGann
Weekdays · 10 AM – 2 PM

Mike carries the middays on Legends 100.3, keeping the Great American Songbook playing straight through the middle of the day.

SK
Weekday Afternoons

Steve Ketelaar

Legends Afternoons with Steve Ketelaar
Weekdays · 2–7 PM

Steve rides the afternoon drive on Legends 100.3, carrying the standards from mid-afternoon through the evening commute.

AD
Weekday Evenings

Alex Donner

Evenings with Alex Donner
Weekdays · 7–9 PM

Alex hosts the evening hours on Legends 100.3 — supper-club standards for the front end of the night.

BM
Weeknights & Sundays

Bob Merrill

Legends After Dark
Weeknights · 9–11 PM

Bob hosts Legends After Dark on weeknights and returns for the Sunday Legends Brunch.

DR
Founder · Late Nights

Dick Robinson

American Standards by the Sea
Weeknights · 11 PM – 1 AM

Founder of Legends Radio and of the Connecticut School of Broadcasting. His American Standards by the Sea is the station's flagship programme, syndicated well beyond the Palm Beaches.
