Signature Programs

Shows worth setting your day around

Weekdays · 6–10 AM

The Morning Lounge

Jill & Rich Switzer

Wake up in the Palm Beaches with live music, warm conversation, and the Songbook's brightest mornings.

Weekdays · 10 AM–2 PM

Middays with Walt Pinto

Walt Pinto

The full music library, wide open — plus the daily Legends at Lunch at noon.

Weekdays · 2–6 PM

Afternoons with Lorna O'Connell

Lorna O'Connell

Timeless company from the afternoon through the golden-hour drive home.

Sat 6–8 PM · Sun 10 AM–12 PM

American Standards by the Sea

Dick Robinson

Dick Robinson's internationally-syndicated salute to Sinatra, Bennett, Streisand and more — produced in part aboard his yacht Airwaves and heard on 75+ stations.

Sundays · 8–10 PM

Legends of Jazz

Gregory “Popeye” Alexander

The Songbook's cooler, smokier side — jazz vocals and instrumentals with a real player at the helm.

Sundays · 7–7:30 AM

The Golf & Travel Show

Dan Shube & Doris Muscarella

Destinations, equipment, and expert interviews for the golfer and traveler in all of us.

Weeknights · 11 PM–1 AM

Late Nights with Legends

Dick Robinson

Encore hours of American Standards by the Sea to carry you into the small hours.

Weekly · See schedule

Inspired To Be

Sherrye Fenton

Uplifting conversation and guests — a reminder that Legends is about companionship as much as music.

Saturdays · 8:30 AM

Pain 2 Power

Mike McCannon & David Kashuba

A weekly dose of health, healing, and the mindset to turn pain into power — Legends' hometown wellness half-hour.

Weekdays · 12 PM

Legends at Lunch

Daily at noon

A midday set of the greatest recordings ever made — the perfect soundtrack to your lunch hour.
