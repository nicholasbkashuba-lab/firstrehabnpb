Knee · Structure

Medial collateral ligament (MCL)

The MCL runs along the inner side of your knee and keeps it from buckling inward. It's the knee's main defense against a force coming from the outside.

What Can Go Wrong

MCL sprain or tear

MCL injuries typically follow a blow to the outside of the knee or a twist, leaving the inner knee tender and sore. Because the MCL has a good blood supply, it often heals well.

How we treat itMost MCL sprains recover with physical therapy and, when helpful, a supportive brace — we restore motion first, then rebuild strength and stability.

Knee pain rarely fixes itself — and most of it responds to skilled physical therapy. Our therapists evaluate your knee and build a plan around your goals.
