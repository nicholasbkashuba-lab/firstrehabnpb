## 01 put the MRI down (RAW 17:58 to 18:31)

"When a patient comes in with an MRI, put it down."

Neurologist Dr. Reed Stone says the scan is the last thing he looks at. Dave agrees: "I do not do treatment on an MRI." Some people with full thickness rotator cuff tears lift their arm fine, and some with a small tear can't.

Talk to your own doctor about your imaging and your symptoms.
