## Title

Episode 21: Dave and Mike's Halloween Special

## Description

```
It's Halloween, so Dave and Mike swap places. Mike opens in Dave's First Rehab shirt, Dave shows up in Mike's red sequin jacket and sunglasses, and for a few minutes nobody is quite sure who is who.

Then they get to the question listeners keep asking: what comes next after you have beaten breast cancer? Dave walks through why exercise matters so much once treatment ends, how 150 to 300 minutes a week breaks down to about 30 minutes a day, and why starting small is fine. Fifty steps today, sixty tomorrow. He covers filling two thirds of the plate with colorful, non starchy vegetables, why weight training matters for bone health after treatment, and a simple routine you can start at home with a chair and a towel.

Mike asks the question everyone has about alkaline water, Dave explains how the body keeps its own pH in check, and the two of them land on the advice that actually helps: hydrate. Along the way you get the history of Halloween costumes, why laughter is good medicine, Mike's story of the wrist that finally stopped hurting, and Dave's skeleton joke.

Next week: neurologist Dr. Reed Stone.

Pain 2 Power airs Saturdays at 8:30 AM on 100.3 Legends Radio. First Rehabilitation of North Palm Beach, 733 US Highway 1, Suite 2A, North Palm Beach. 561-624-4263, www.firstrehabnpb.com

General information, not medical advice. Talk to your own doctor before starting an exercise program after cancer treatment.
```
