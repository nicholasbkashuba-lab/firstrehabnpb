# Hashtags, week of Sun Nov 1 to Sun Nov 8, 2026

Researched 2026-10-30 (Nick asked that day). Rules: `README.md` in this folder.

## What the research found

- **Instagram caps hashtags at 5** on posts and Reels (announced 2025-12-18). Two scheduled posts were over it
  (Nov 1 "November in North County" and the Nov 6 Stone announcement, 6 each); both fixed.
- **TikTok Creative Center, US, last 7 days, Health industry view:** #tiktokscaryhorrorcontest (a TikTok film
  contest), #marvelrivalsavengers (games), #livecanbeeasy. Nothing that fits a health podcast clip; not used.
  Tokchart's worldwide daily list (Oct 30) was sports and foreign TV; not used.
- **November observances:** National Diabetes Month (Nov 1 to 30; no official hashtag from NIDDK or ADA, the
  common tag is `#NationalDiabetesMonth`); National Family Caregivers Month (`#NFCMonth`, used by ACL and CAN);
  Movember / men's health (`#Movember`); World Diabetes Day Nov 14.
- **Seasonal:** clocks fall back Sunday Nov 1 (`#FallBack`); Halloween tags still carry for the first days.
- 2026 guidance (marketing guides, not platform documentation): 3 to 5 tags on TikTok and Reels, niche over
  generic, rotate sets; YouTube leans on title and description more than tags.
