## Description

```
"When a patient comes in with an MRI, put it down." Neurologist Dr. Reed Stone of Palm Beach Neurology joins Dr. Dave Kashuba and Mike McGann. Dave has worked with him since 1990, and Dr. Stone has been doing nerve testing for over 40 years.

He explains what an EMG and a nerve conduction study actually feel like today, from the autoclaved needles of the early days to fine needles that work like microphones in the muscle. He talks about why the history and physical still come before any test or scan, how a patient set for spine surgery can have a neuropathy in their feet that nobody caught, and what a first visit with him looks like.

Dr. Stone has also been a patient at First Rehab. Years of weightlifting left him with shoulder pain that kept him out of the gym, and he talks about getting back to training with his son Zander with no surgery. Dave tells the story of a friend diagnosed with ALS whose testing found Kennedy's disease, and Mike shares how his mother's Charcot was finally diagnosed.
```
