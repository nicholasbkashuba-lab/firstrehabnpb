## Description

```
It's Halloween, so Dave and Mike swap places: Mike in Dave's First Rehab shirt, Dave in Mike's red sequin jacket and sunglasses. Then they get to the question listeners keep asking: what comes next after you have beaten breast cancer?

Dr. Dave Kashuba walks through why exercise matters so much once treatment ends, how 150 to 300 minutes a week breaks down to about 30 minutes a day, and why starting small is fine. He covers filling two thirds of your plate with colorful, non starchy vegetables, why weight training matters for bone health after treatment, and a simple routine you can start at home with a chair and a towel. Mike asks about alkaline water, Dave explains how your body keeps its own pH in check, and the advice they land on is the simple one: hydrate.

Plus the history of Halloween costumes, why laughter is good medicine, Mike's wrist story and Dave's skeleton joke.
```
