## Description

```
Could diet and exercise really change your breast cancer risk? Following last week's episode on breast cancer screening, Dr. Dave Kashuba and Mike McGann sit down with registered dietitian Elyse Marrone and Pilates Life owner Danielle Armstrong to talk about what you can control.

Elyse explains the seven pillars of health she works through with every patient (food first, exercise, sleep, stress management, supplementation, hydration and community) and why, after a diagnosis, the first place she looks is the gut. Danielle shares how Pilates has helped her clients in their 60s and 70s stay strong through treatment, and tells the story of Joseph Pilates and Eve Gentry, the dancer he helped regain her full range of motion after a radical mastectomy.

Also in this episode: Lolita San Miguel, the last living elder who trained with Joseph Pilates, who lives right here in Juno Beach; Brussels sprouts the Polish way; deep sleep and sleep trackers; and why blue light glasses after 8 PM can help you fall asleep faster.
```
