# "This month in North County" posts

One social post a month listing a handful of things happening around North County, so the
clinic's feed carries something local and useful, not only clinic news. Nick asked for it
2026-10-26 ("do both", alongside the seasonal list on /things-to-do.html). The monthly
routine (`docs/routines/monthly-north-county.md`) drafts and schedules it; Nick reviews it
in Post Bridge before it goes out, the same as every other scheduled post.

One file per month: `content/monthly/YYYY-MM.md`, holding the caption, every source URL,
and the Post Bridge post ids once scheduled.

## Slot

The **1st of the month, 10:30 AM ET**. Clear of the 9:00 AM daily clip and Google Business
post and the Friday noon dad joke. Scheduled about a week ahead so Nick has time to look.

## Platforms

Facebook 81324, Instagram 81353, LinkedIn business 81322, plus a separate Google Business
81642 call (text and ONE image, LEARN_MORE CTA). Never LinkedIn personal 81320. Always run
`list_social_accounts` first; ids change on every reconnect.
Instagram collaborators: none. This is not a Pain 2 Power post.

## Image

One still from Nick's OWN footage, never a Commons photo (those need a credit line a social
image cannot carry cleanly) and never an operator's photo. Rotate through
`assets/places/juno-pier-1600.jpg`, `assets/places/lighthouse-1600.jpg` and
`assets/media/ttd-hero-juno-poster.jpg`. Reuse the Post Bridge media id recorded in the last
month's file instead of re-uploading (uploads are metered).

## Rules

- **Every event is verified on its OWN official page**: the organizer, the city, town,
  village or county, the venue, or the nonprofit that runs it. Never an aggregator, news
  story, ticket reseller or tourism listicle. If the official page will not open, drop the
  event. floridastateparks.org refuses automated reads, so state park events are out unless
  another official page states them.
- **Exact dates as the official page states them.** No "every Saturday" unless the page says
  so. Re-check anything that has been rescheduled or cancelled.
- **4 to 6 events**, spread across the month and across towns, West Palm Beach to Hobe Sound.
  Favor free, outdoor, active or family events (walks, runs, markets, festivals, tours,
  concerts in a park). Nothing political, nothing with alcohol as the point of it.
- **Zero dashes** outside 561-624-4263 and 561-624-GAME. Bullets use `•`. Same as every caption.
- **Lead with the month**, one line on getting out and moving, then the bullets
  (`• Nov 7 · West Palm Beach GreenMarket · Saturdays downtown`), then "Hours and details
  change, so check before you go," then the link.
- **Link**: `https://www.firstrehabnpb.com/things-to-do.html?utm_source={facebook|linkedin}&utm_medium=social&utm_campaign=monthly`.
  Instagram captions are not clickable, so it says "link in bio". Google Business CTA URL ends
  `?utm_source=google&utm_medium=gbp&utm_campaign=monthly`.
- We are not affiliated with any of these events. Never imply a partnership or sponsorship.
- Google Business caption: 750 to 1,200 characters, text plus the one image.
- Finish with `list_posts` showing each post scheduled. "processing" is not proof of anything.
