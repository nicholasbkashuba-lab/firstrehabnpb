November in North County

The mornings are finally cooler, which makes this a great month to get outside and move. A few things happening around us:

• Saturdays · West Palm Beach GreenMarket on the waterfront, 8:30 a.m. to 1:30 p.m.
• Sundays · The Gardens GreenMarket at Palm Beach Gardens City Hall, 8 a.m. to 1 p.m.
• Nov 7 · Arts & Crafts Fall Festival at the North Palm Beach Community Center. Free, 9 a.m. to 4 p.m.
• Nov 11 · Veterans Day ceremonies in North Palm Beach (9 a.m.), Jupiter (9 a.m.) and Palm Beach Gardens (11 a.m.)
• Nov 13 and 14 · Gardens Harvest and Holiday Bazaar at the Burns Road Community Center. Free.
• Wednesdays · Sunset tours at the Jupiter Inlet Lighthouse, plus a Moonrise Tour on Nov 24. Tickets needed.
• Nov 27 · Holiday in Paradise opens on the West Palm Beach waterfront and runs through Dec 31.

Hours and details change, so check before you go. More parks, beaches and trails from West Palm Beach to Hobe Sound: https://www.firstrehabnpb.com/things-to-do.html?utm_source=facebook&utm_medium=social&utm_campaign=monthly
