Condition pages: clinical review

39 condition pages are built. 39 are waiting for a clinician. A page stays hidden from Google, and unlinked from the public site, until it is reviewed.

How to review a page

Open the page, read it as you would a patient handout, and check the list under its name below. Send corrections to Nick. When a page is right, tell Nick your name and the date, and he adds it to CONDITION_REVIEW in build.py. That one line publishes the page, links it from its body area page, and shows "Clinically reviewed by" with your name.
