## 02-gasket.mp4

The labrum, think of it like a gasket. If you have two pipes, you're thinking of a gasket that's creating a seal. For the hip, it creates negative pressure so that when you walk, it spreads the contact pressure, the weight, throughout the whole dome. And if you lose that suction seal, you start increasing pressure on the cartilage in there. Think about what your torso weighs, the top half of your body, and the weight those joints carry every step. If that force isn't being distributed equally along that dome, it's going to wear away so quickly. You're asking it to carry so much more weight at specifically targeted areas. Especially if the labrum's involved, it's going to hurt every step. You're kind of dragging your leg behind you. So it's very painful.
