# What the patient data says, and what to do about it (2026-10)

Aggregates only, from a SPRY patient export (1,396 records, 1,043 with visits) and SPRY report
screenshots Nick supplied in October 2026. No patient-level data is, or may ever be, in this repo.

## The numbers

| Signal | Value |
|---|---|
| Age | Median about 71; 67% are 65+ (65-74: 30%, 75-84: 27%, 85+: 11%); 55-64: 16% |
| Sex | 57% female |
| Payer, share of patients | Medicare about 48% (and about 50% of visits); commercial plus Medicare Advantage about 34%; auto / PI / network about 5%; blank 13% |
| Visits per patient | 13.3 overall; auto / PI 24.6; OT 15.6 |
| OT | About 12% of patients; 41% of OT patients are working age |
| Home zip codes (Florida patients) | 33408 North Palm Beach / Juno 36%; 33410 + 33418 Palm Beach Gardens 23%; 33404 Riviera Beach / Singer Island 14%; Jupiter about 8% |
| Seasonal | About 5.5% list an out-of-state address; GBP calls peak in January (147 in Jan 2026 vs 88 to 110 May to Sep) |
| Referrals | About 89% of appointments carry a referring physician in SPRY; 279 referring doctors; 73% sent one patient; 18 sent five or more; the top 10 send 23% |
| Drop-off | 16% of treated patients stop at three visits or fewer; 47% end "inactive" rather than discharged |
| Evaluations | Initial evaluations are 7.8% of about 14,700 completed appointments (about 22 a week) |
| Price | Average paid per visit: self-pay $77.24 vs insurance $73.35 |

Caveats: cancellation counts include reschedules, so they are not a loss measure. Most visits
are booked under David and Kayla for convenience, so per-provider numbers mean nothing.

## The connections, and the action each one points to

1. **Two thirds are 65+ and half are Medicare.** Medicare clarity is the conversion lever.
   Shipped: the 2026 Medicare guide on /insurance.html. Next: deepen balance and fall
   prevention on existing pages (the strength-after-60 post, the wellness page), and make
   "we verify your Medicare and Medigap before your first visit" visible on every service page.
2. **Physicians drive almost everything.** With 89% physician-referred and 73% of referrers
   sending one patient, the biggest growth lever is converting one-time referrers into repeat
   ones, not search. Start with the 18 doctors who sent five or more and the 58 who sent two to
   four: a visit, a one-page "what we do and how to refer" sheet, and an invitation to the
   November event. Pain 2 Power guests are warm relationships already.
3. **Auto / PI patients are worth almost twice a typical patient** (24.6 visits vs 13.3). The
   auto-accident page sits around position 30. Deepen it (PIP basics, documentation for
   attorneys, what the first 14 days mean in Florida, which a page can state with an official
   source) and build an attorney referral list the same way as the physician list.
4. **OT patients stay longest and 41% are working age.** Return-to-work, workers' comp and
   ergonomic work are the commercial angle. Deepen /services/occupational-therapy.html and
   /treatments/workers-comp.html for those searches; reach out to local employers' HR and
   workers' comp adjusters.
5. **Riviera Beach / Singer Island is 14% of patients**, second only to home, yet city pages
   earn no clicks. That market is won in the Google Business Profile (service area, reviews
   that mention Singer Island naturally), not with more page copy.
6. **Season.** GBP calls run about 50% higher in January. Front-load reviews, GBP posts and
   physician visits in November and December so the listing is at its strongest when
   snowbirds search.
7. **16% stop by visit three; 47% drift off without a discharge.** A visit-three check-in call
   and a 90-day reactivation call list (built from SPRY, never stored here) protect revenue
   already won. Every discharged patient is also the review request.
8. **Leads arrive without insurance and without outcomes.** Make insurance a required choice
   on the appointment form so the front desk can verify before calling back, and have the
   front desk mark lead outcomes so the site learns which pages produce patients.
9. **Self-pay barely beats insurance per visit.** A pricing review is overdue (owner decision;
   not shown on the site by Nick's choice).
