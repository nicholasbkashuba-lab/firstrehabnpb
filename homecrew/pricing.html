What is not on this page

Setup fees, cancellation fees and per-report charges, because we do not have any. The monthly price is the price for the visits listed. Anything outside that we quote before we do it.

Which package do most people take

Gold, among owners who leave for the whole summer — largely because of the arrival preparation and the storm work rather than the inspections themselves. Owners who are away for shorter stretches, or who have family nearby, tend to sit on Bronze or Silver.

We would rather put you on the right package than the expensive one. Call and describe how you actually use the house.
