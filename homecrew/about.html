Our Story

Firefighter owned & operated

Built on a foundation of service, integrity and attention to detail — by people trained to notice what is wrong before it becomes an emergency.

Why a firefighter ends up doing this

The job trains a particular habit: walk into a building, read it quickly, and notice the thing that is about to become a much bigger problem. Discoloured ceiling. Smell that is not right. A sound the building should not be making.

That habit turns out to be exactly what an empty house needs from whoever is checking on it. Most of what destroys a vacant Florida home announces itself well before it does the damage — but only to somebody who is looking for it, on a schedule, and knows what they are looking at.

What that means for your house

  • The same twenty five checks every visit, so nothing depends on who felt thorough that day
  • A person whose name is on the report, not an anonymous company
  • Somebody who is genuinely comfortable being called at 2am, because that is the job they already have
  • Fully insured and bonded — proof of coverage available before you sign anything

Local, and staying that way

We work out of Stuart and cover five counties from Indian River down to Broward. That is a deliberate limit. It is far enough to serve the coast properly and close enough that we can actually get to a property when something goes wrong, which is the entire point of hiring somebody local instead of an app.

We would rather tell you we cannot cover an address than stretch the footprint and do it badly. If you are outside the five counties, say so when you call and we will be straight with you.

The standard

Professional. Reliable. Local. It is on the bottom of every page and the bottom of every report because it is the whole promise: show up when we said, do the same thorough job every time, and be a real person you can reach.
