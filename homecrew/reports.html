How the score works

Each of the twenty five lines is marked OK, Watch or Issue. Each of the five systems is scored out of 100, and those roll into the Overall Home Health Score.

The score exists to make one visit comparable to the last one. A home that has sat at 100 for four months and comes in at 82 is telling you something specific, and the report says exactly which lines moved and why.

Attention items carry their own evidence

Anything marked Watch or Issue gets a photo and a written note attached to that line, not to the report in general. A photo of the actual stain, sitting directly under “visible leaks”, tells you something that the same photo in a grid at the bottom of the page does not.

Nothing about your gate code, lock box combination or alarm ever appears in a report. Those live in the crew portal and stay there. A report gets emailed and forwarded onward — an access code in one is an access code in somebody's inbox forever.

Why the documentation matters

Many Florida policies limit or exclude water damage coverage in a home left unoccupied beyond a set number of days unless it is being checked on a documented schedule. A timestamped report with photos is that record. Ask your carrier what interval your policy requires — we are not insurance advisors, but we will happily send your agent a sample report.

How it reaches you

By email, after every visit. If something needs your attention today you get a phone call as well — not just a line in a document you might read on Sunday.
