Service Area

Home watch in Indian River County

The north end of our run. Vero Beach and Sebastian sit far enough up the coast that a lot of home watch companies quietly stop before they get there.

Communities we cover

Vero Beach, Sebastian, Wabasso, Orchid, Indian River Shores and the surrounding area. If your address is in Indian River County and it is not on that list, call and ask — the list is examples, not limits.

Barrier island properties here take the brunt of salt air. Exterior surfaces and window seals get extra attention on the sweep.

Same protocol, wherever the house is

A property in Indian River gets exactly the same five-system, 25-point sweep as one three doors from our office in Stuart, and the same report format afterwards. Being further from home base changes our drive time. It does not change the service.

Most owners here run weekly visits through the months they are away, which is the interval most Florida carriers and home watch standards point to. Ask your insurer what your policy requires and we will build the schedule around it.

Every Visit

What we check in Indian River

The same twenty five lines, in the same order, cleared in the field with a photo and a note on anything flagged.

System 01

Security

  • Doors & locks
  • Windows & sliders
  • Garage & overhead
  • Signs of forced entry
  • Alarm & camera status
System 02

Plumbing

  • Visible leaks
  • Water pressure
  • Water heater
  • Drains & traps
  • Toilets flushed, faucets run
System 03

HVAC

  • Cooling operation
  • Thermostat setpoint
  • Air filter condition
  • Interior humidity reading
  • Condensate line
System 04

Exterior

  • Roof line (visual)
  • Pool & spa
  • Landscaping
  • Irrigation
  • Exterior surfaces
System 05

Storm Readiness

  • Shutters & panels
  • Outdoor furniture secured
  • Storm supplies on hand
  • Drains & gutters clear
  • Generator & fuel
