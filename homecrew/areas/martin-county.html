Service Area

Home watch in Martin County

Home base. Stuart, Palm City, Jensen Beach, Hobe Sound and Sewall's Point are the shortest drive we make, which means the fastest response when something goes wrong.

Communities we cover

Stuart, Palm City, Jensen Beach, Hobe Sound, Sewall's Point, Rio and the surrounding area. If your address is in Martin County and it is not on that list, call and ask — the list is examples, not limits.

Being local is the whole product. When an alarm trips at 2am in Martin County, we are minutes away rather than hours.

Same protocol, wherever the house is

A property in Martin gets exactly the same five-system, 25-point sweep as one three doors from our office in Stuart, and the same report format afterwards. Being further from home base changes our drive time. It does not change the service.

Most owners here run weekly visits through the months they are away, which is the interval most Florida carriers and home watch standards point to. Ask your insurer what your policy requires and we will build the schedule around it.

Every Visit

What we check in Martin

The same twenty five lines, in the same order, cleared in the field with a photo and a note on anything flagged.

Security

The fastest part of the walk and the one where a problem is most obvious, which is exactly why it goes first.

  • Doors & locks
  • Windows & sliders
  • Garage & overhead
  • Signs of forced entry
  • Alarm & camera status

Plumbing

Running every tap sounds trivial and is not. P-traps hold a plug of water that stops sewer gas coming back up the drain, and in a hot empty house that water evaporates in weeks.

  • Visible leaks
  • Water pressure
  • Water heater
  • Drains & traps
  • Toilets flushed, faucets run

HVAC

Humidity is the number that predicts trouble in a Florida house, and the condensate line is the component most likely to quietly take the whole system out of service.

  • Cooling operation
  • Thermostat setpoint
  • Air filter condition
  • Interior humidity reading
  • Condensate line

Exterior

Pool water level is worth watching closely. A pool dropping faster than evaporation explains is telling you something.

  • Roof line (visual)
  • Pool & spa
  • Landscaping
  • Irrigation
  • Exterior surfaces

Storm Readiness

Checked year round rather than in June, because the time to discover a seized shutter track is not the day a storm is named.

  • Shutters & panels
  • Outdoor furniture secured
  • Storm supplies on hand
  • Drains & gutters clear
  • Generator & fuel
