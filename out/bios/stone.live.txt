A graduate of Brooklyn College (Biology, Magna Cum Laude), he earned his medical degree with honors from Universidad Central Del Este in the Dominican Republic and completed his neurology residency at SUNY Downstate Medical Center, where he served as Chief Resident. He later joined the faculty at Kings County Hospital and Downstate Medical Center, where he taught, practiced, and served as Director of Neurological Services in the emergency department. ​ Dr. Stone specializes in the diagnosis and treatment of spine, nerve, and muscle disorders, with expertise in EMG-NCV testing. He also has extensive experience in medical-legal neurology, including workers&rsquo; compensation and personal injury cases. ​ &nbsp;Dr. Stone service exceeds 50 years, and is committed to providing the highest level of care. Supported by his wife and three sons, he looks forward to continuing to serve the community for many years to come.
