Lauderdale, Florida. He earned his Bachelor of Science in Biology with honors from Nova Southeastern University, followed by his medical degree from Nova Southeastern University College of Osteopathic Medicine. He completed his neurology residency at Larkin Community Hospital in Miami, where he also served as chief resident. ​ He specializes in the diagnosis and management of neurological disorders in adults, with a subspecialty focus on multiple sclerosis and related neuro-immunological conditions. To further this expertise, he completed fellowship training at the University of Miami, gaining extensive experience in complex cases and advanced therapies. ​ Passionate about continuous learning and patient-centered care, Dr. Coppola is committed to providing individualized treatment, recognizing that medicine is both a science and an art.
