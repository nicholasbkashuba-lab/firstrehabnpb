### 1. GBP call clicks down 42% — worse than the −7 to −17% flagged 2026-09-03, needs a human look

**Proposed fix:** none from here — this is a "go look at the dashboard" item, not a code or copy fix.

### 2. `lead_submit` is undercounting real leads by ~10x
21 real leads landed in Supabase this window; the `lead_submit` tracking event (added 2026-09-03, fires on a successful chat/contact-form delivery) only fired twice. Something between "lead delivered" and "event recorded" is failing most of the time — candidates: the event only fires on the *first* delivery attempt, so leads that succeed via the offline-retry queue after a failed first try may take a different code path; an ad blocker suppressing the `window.va` beacon; or a lead path (e.g. the legacy `/request-appointment` redirect, or a page that doesn't load `intake.js`) that was never wired to `trackLeadSent`.

**Proposed fix:** review `assets/js/intake.js`'s `trackLeadSent` call sites against every path that can produce a row in `intake_leads`, and check whether the contact-page static form specifically is one of the 21 (it carries the majority of leads per the 2026-09-03 analysis, so if its tracking is the gap, that explains most of the shortfall). Left as a proposal — this is a diagnosis job, not a one-line fix.
