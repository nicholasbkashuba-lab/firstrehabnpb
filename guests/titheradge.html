Captain Kerry Titheradge

Captain on Bravo’s Below Deck

On the show

“It’s very little effort for me to get in a car and drive an hour… just to help others out.”

Captain Kerry first met Dave more than 20 years ago as a patient with a rotator cuff injury, and credits Dave and his team with getting his shoulder back after two later surgeries. He talked about his own podcast, where he interviews well known people to show the struggles behind their success, and his Mental Health Mondays. He also described how a birthday toy drive at a watch party during his first season showed him what the platform could do, and the dog rescue and veterans charities he now supports.

Background

  • He was born in Brisbane, Australia, and has worked on boats for about 30 years, about two decades of that on yachts.
  • He captained the inaugural charter of Below Deck Adventure, which premiered on Bravo in November 2022.
  • He took the helm of Below Deck for Season 11 aboard the motor yacht St. David, chartering in Grenada.
  • He is back as captain for Below Deck Season 13, which premiered in October 2026.
  • He is the owner and CEO of Yachting Concepts, a yacht management company that helps new yacht owners find their way in the industry.
  • Off the boat, he rescues huskies.
