What We Treat

Overview of Treatments

Twelve specialized treatment pathways — every one beginning with a thorough evaluation and a plan built for you.

Lower Back & Spine

Back Pain Relief

Evidence-based physical therapy for low back pain, sciatica, and spinal conditions — treating the cause, not just the symptoms.

Head & Neck

Neck Pain Relief

Manual therapy, posture correction, and strengthening for neck pain, stiffness, and whiplash.

Shoulder

Shoulder Pain Relief

Specialized rehabilitation for rotator cuff injuries, frozen shoulder, and everything in between.

Knee

Knee Pain Relief

From arthritis to ACL recovery — rebuild strong, confident knees.

Hip & Pelvis

Hip Pain Relief

Restore pain-free walking, standing, and living with targeted hip rehabilitation.

Foot

Foot Pain Relief

Get back on your feet — literally — with expert care for plantar fasciitis and foot pain.

Ankle

Ankle Pain Relief

Rebuild stability and strength after sprains, fractures, and chronic ankle problems.

Wrist & Hand

Hand & Wrist Therapy

Certified hand therapy for the intricate mechanics of your hands and wrists.

Head & Neck

Headache Relief

Physical therapy for cervicogenic headaches and tension-type headaches that start in the neck.

Full Body

Post-Surgical Rehabilitation

Structured, surgeon-coordinated recovery from joint replacement, spine surgery, and beyond.

Work Injury

Workers' Compensation Rehab

Get better and get back to work — with the documentation and communication your case requires.

Full Body

Auto Accident Recovery

Comprehensive rehabilitation after a car accident — from whiplash to complex multi-area injuries.
